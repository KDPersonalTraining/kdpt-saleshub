People get stuck in the gym for four reasons: they don't train hard enough, they rush their reps, they never progress, and they have no proof anything's working. This programme fixes one each week:

•  Week 1, Calibrate: learn what hard actually feels like

•  Week 2, Control: slow down and make every rep count

•  Week 3, Overload: add weight with a simple rule

•  Week 4, Prove it: retest and see the numbers

You'll train 3 times a week. Each session has a job: A builds strength, B builds muscle, C fixes weak links.

Log every weight and rep in your tracker. That's how we'll prove you're not stuck.

Today: Session A, Strength (Week 1)

Warm up: 5 min bike or rower, then 2 lighter sets of squats.

It opens your Unstuck tracker: every exercise, your sets and reps, and why each one is in the programme. Log each set as you go and it saves on your phone. From next week, your previous numbers sit right next to each exercise.

This week's rule: on the last set of the first three exercises, push until you have only 1 good rep left. Most people stop with 5 or more left without realising. That gap is why they're stuck.
