Here's the rule that will keep you progressing long after these 30 days:

1.Pick a rep range (say 8 to 10).

2.Use the same weight until you hit the top of the range on every set.

3.Then add a small amount of weight and start again at the bottom.

If you added weight yesterday and couldn't hit the reps, that's fine. Keep the weight, get one more rep next time.

That's it. It works for beginners and for people who've trained for years. Most people get stuck because they never follow it.
