Getting Back Into Routine, Gently

If summer pulled you away from your usual routine, that's normal. It doesn't mean you've lost anything, you just need one small step in the right direction, not a big reset.

  • ✓ Book your first session in like an appointment you can't miss

03Myth, Busted

"If you're not sore the next day, the workout didn't work."

This is one of the things I hear most. Soreness just means your muscles did something unfamiliar, not that the session worked. The more used to a movement you get, the less sore you'll feel doing it, even as you keep getting stronger.

Chasing soreness leads people to either overtrain trying to "earn" it, or feel like they've failed after a good session that didn't leave them aching. Neither helps. Track progress by what you can lift and whether you're showing up, not how wrecked you feel the next day.

02Nutrition Tip

The Simplest Fix for Autumn Energy Dips

As the days get shorter, a lot of people notice their energy dropping and reach for quick carbs to fix it. Usually the bigger issue is protein and fibre being too low earlier in the day.

  • ✓ Get a solid source of protein at breakfast, not just lunch and dinner
  • ✓ Add a source of fibre earlier in the day, fruit, oats, or veg
  • ✓ Notice the afternoon slump less when breakfast actually holds you

01Staying Consistent

How to Keep Training When It's Dark at 4pm

Motivation is always lowest when the weather's bad and the evenings are dark. Rather than waiting until you feel like it, make it easier to just turn up.

  • ✓ Pick your session times now and put them in your diary like any other appointment
  • ✓ Lay your kit out the night before, or pack your gym bag before work
  • ✓ Train earlier in the day where you can, before the dark evenings take over
  • ✓ Keep a 20 minute option for tired days, a short session still counts
  • ✓ Warm up a few minutes longer in the cold, your body takes longer to get going
02Home Workout

A 30 Minute Workout You Can Do at Home

No gym or equipment needed, so it works on the nights you can't get out. Do 4 rounds, resting 60 to 90 seconds between each round.

  • ✓ 12 bodyweight squats
  • ✓ 10 press-ups, on your knees or with your hands raised if you need to
  • ✓ 10 reverse lunges on each leg
  • ✓ 15 glute bridges
  • ✓ 30 second plank

Keep your form tidy and move at a steady pace. When it starts to feel easy, add a round or a bit of weight.

03Nutrition Tip

Winter Eating Without Losing Your Progress

Cold weather makes us want heavier, comforting food, and that's fine. You don't have to give it up, just build it around protein.

  • ✓ Batch cook a chilli, stew or curry and portion it up for the week
  • ✓ Add protein to soups, like chicken, beans or lentils
  • ✓ Stir a scoop of protein powder into your porridge for a warm, filling breakfast
  • ✓ Keep frozen veg in the freezer so adding it takes no effort
